About the Archive

Recovered fragments from a vanished screen civilization.

The Cache preserved selected artefacts from the early twenty-first century internet, a dispersed civilization whose citizens entrusted memory, status, grief, commerce, and lunch documentation to unstable platform-sites.

Our department maintained that these fragments deserved patient classification. Though many records arrived corrupted, repetitive, or attached to improbable dances, the archive treated each inscription as evidence of a people attempting to be known by machines and one another.
